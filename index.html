Week 1 (May 20)

What's for Lunch? (Student Book pages 104, 105, 106)

CNB Guatemala & Learning Goal: Express comprehension of informational texts about world foods, identifying main ideas and practicing vocabulary with proper pronunciation.

🌟 Leader in Me Habit:

Habit 3: Put First Things First (Prioritize tasks to read carefully and complete workbook activities on time).

🚀 Phase I: Introduction & Warm-Up (~5 mins)

Brain Activation & Vocabulary

1. Vocabulary Review (Units 1 & 2 + Unit 3 Preview)

Teacher leads a quick flashcard review on screen: words related to food, places, and daily routines. Students chant words aloud in pairs (e.g., cafeteria, soup, beans, rice, healthy, exercise).

2. Engaging Hook / Real-World Connection

Teacher asks: "What did you eat for lunch today? Do children around the world eat the same things?" Short popcorn sharing with the class.

Materials: Digital projector, Student Book Our World 3, whiteboard markers.

✍️ Phase II: Individual Practice (20 mins)

Independent Production

Reading & Comprehension Task (Student Book / Workbook)

Students read the assigned pages independently and complete the True/False check and chart completion exercises in their books. Focus on finding text evidence without copying from peers.

Answer Key Guidance for Teacher:

• True/False Answers: 1. False, 2. True, 3. True, 4. True, 5. True (based on text).

• Chart Completion: France (cafeteria, big lunch / fish, sausages, vegetables); Russia (soup, fish/meat, bread); Japan (classroom, soup, rice/noodles, fish); Brazil (rice, beans, salad, meat).

🤝 Phase III: Cooperative / Team Work (30 mins)

Peer Interaction & Synergy

Team Structure & Roles

Working in pairs or small groups of 3. Assign roles: Leader (keeps group on task), Reader (reads prompts), Recorder (writes down ideas).

Leader in Me Connection — Habit 6: Sinergizar

"Together is better." Teams collaborate to discuss questions, compare their answers, and prepare a joint mini-presentation.

Concrete Team Product

Create a comparative chart or role-play dialogue (e.g., discussing favorite meals with a partner just like in SB page 105, activity 4).

🎯 Phase IV: Plenary, Interactive Quiz & Home Extension (15 mins)

Closure & Evaluation

Interactive Trivia / Pasapalabra Mini-Game

Teacher launches a quick digital vocabulary & reading trivia based on the lesson. Teams raise hands to answer for bonus points.

💡 Sample Question: "Where do children in Japan usually eat lunch?" -> Answer: "In the classroom!"

🏡 Take-Home Investigation Activity

Interview a family member at home: "What was your favorite lunch when you were 8 years old?" Bring 3 bullet points of notes to share next class.
